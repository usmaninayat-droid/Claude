How real products hide & show selection checkboxes

Four patterns that leading tools use to keep checkboxes hidden until needed — exactly the opt-in behaviour your ticket asks for. Each demo below works: interact with it to watch the checkboxes appear and disappear. Source link under each.

Notion · Google DriveHover-reveal

The row shows a file/type icon by default. Hover a row and the icon swaps into a checkbox. No button, no wasted column — the checkbox lives exactly where the icon was. Header select-all is faint until hovered.

iOS Mail · Gmail mobileSelect button

No checkboxes at rest. A Select button reveals a checkbox on every row at once and enters selection mode; Cancel hides them again. The clearest opt-in on touch, where there's no hover.
